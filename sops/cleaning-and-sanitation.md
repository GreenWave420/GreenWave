# SOP: Cleaning & Sanitation

**Doc ID:** SOP-CLN-001 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Version:** 1.0 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Owner:** QA / GM
**Effective date:** ____________ &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Review due:** annually

---

## 1. Purpose
To keep the facility free of pests, pathogens, cross-contamination and residue,
protecting crop health and product quality, and to provide auditable evidence
of hygiene control.

## 2. Scope
All grow rooms, propagation, drying/curing, trim/processing, storage, tool
stations, entryways, drains and shared surfaces.

## 3. Definitions
- **Cleaning** — physical removal of soil, plant debris, dust and organic matter
  (you cannot sanitise a dirty surface).
- **Sanitising** — reducing microbes on a *cleaned* surface to a safe level.
- **Disinfecting** — higher-level kill for high-risk situations (disease
  outbreak, room turnover).

## 4. Key principles
1. **Clean first, then sanitise.** Organic matter deactivates most sanitisers.
2. **Clean to dirty, top to bottom.** Work from the cleanest area/room to the
   dirtiest and from high surfaces down to the floor.
3. **Directional flow.** Move people, tools and materials one way: Mothers →
   Veg → Flower → Post-harvest. Never carry tools back "up" without sanitising.
4. **Contact time matters.** Leave sanitiser on the surface for the full
   label-stated dwell time before wiping/drying.
5. **Dry surfaces resist microbes.** Eliminate standing water and puddles.

## 5. Approved agents (confirm your own list & rates)
| Use | Example agent | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| General surface clean | Neutral detergent | Rinse before sanitising |
| Surface sanitiser | Isopropyl alcohol 70% / quaternary ammonium | Dwell time per label |
| Tool sterilisation | Isopropyl 70–99% / dilute bleach / heat | Between plants for cutting tools |
| Root-zone / equipment disinfect | Hydrogen peroxide / horticultural H₂O₂ | Reservoirs, lines |
| Drains | Approved algaecide / peroxide | Prevent biofilm, fungus gnats |

> Only use products approved under your licence for a food/medicine-grade
> cultivation environment. Keep SDS on file. Never mix chemicals (e.g. bleach +
> acid/ammonia).

## 6. Personal hygiene (every entry)
- Clean garments/scrubs; hair (and beard) covered; no jewellery, no nail polish.
- Wash hands with soap, dry, then sanitise. Re-sanitise after touching face,
  waste, floor, or moving between rooms.
- Gloves for handling plants/product; change when torn or between rooms/batches.
- Dedicated or sanitised footwear; use footbath/mat on entry.
- No eating, drinking or smoking in production areas.
- Anyone unwell (respiratory/skin infection) must not handle plants/product.

## 7. Procedures

### 7.1 Daily
- Wipe/sanitise work surfaces, tables and high-touch points (handles, switches,
  controllers, meters) after use.
- Sweep and mop floors; remove all plant debris to lined waste bins.
- Clean tools and return to designated storage; sanitise cutting tools between
  uses.
- Mop up any water/runoff immediately.
- Refill sanitiser stations, soap and paper; re-line bins.

### 7.2 Weekly
- Empty rooms of movable items; wipe and sanitise **all** surfaces incl. under
  benches, corners and skirting.
- Scrub and sanitise floors.
- Clean and sanitise reusable pots/trays/saucers taken out of service.
- Clean drains and dose to prevent biofilm/algae.
- Sanitise propagation domes, cloning gear and trim tables.
- Empty & sanitise dehumidifier/humidifier reservoirs; wipe fan blades/guards.
- Launder or replace garments; restock PPE; refresh footbaths.

### 7.3 Monthly / room turnover (terminal clean)
1. Remove **all** plants, media, pots and equipment.
2. Dry-remove debris (sweep/vacuum) — do not spread dust.
3. Wash all surfaces (ceiling → walls → fixtures → benches → floor) with
   detergent; rinse.
4. Apply disinfectant at correct concentration; observe full dwell time.
5. Clean/replace filters; wipe light hoods; treat drains.
6. Where used, fog/terminal-treat the empty sealed room per approved method.
7. Allow to dry; sanitise before re-introducing clean equipment and plants.
8. Record the clean (room, date, agents, operator, sign-off).

## 8. Colour-coding (recommended)
Assign colour-coded cloths/mops/brushes per zone (e.g. drying vs grow vs
general) to prevent cross-contamination. Store cleaning equipment clean and dry,
off the floor, in a dedicated area — never inside grow rooms.

## 9. Records
Log every scheduled clean: date, area, agent + concentration, dwell time,
operator initials, verification/sign-off. Keep records for the retention period
required by your licence.

## 10. Verification
- Visual inspection after each terminal clean.
- Periodic ATP swabs or environmental monitoring (optional but strong evidence).
- QA spot-audits against this SOP.
