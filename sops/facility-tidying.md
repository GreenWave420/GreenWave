# SOP: Facility Tidying & Housekeeping

**Doc ID:** SOP-HSK-001 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Version:** 1.0 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Owner:** GM
**Effective date:** ____________ &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Review due:** annually

---

## 1. Purpose
An orderly facility is a safe, efficient and compliant one. Tidiness reduces
pest harbourage, prevents accidents, speeds work, and presents well for audits
and inspections. This SOP covers general order and organisation — hygiene
(sanitising) is covered in the [Cleaning & Sanitation SOP](cleaning-and-sanitation.md).

## 2. Scope
All areas: grow rooms, propagation, drying/curing, trim/processing, corridors,
storage, tool stations, office, break room, entryways and exterior immediately
around the building.

## 3. Guiding principle — "5S"
- **Sort** — remove what doesn't belong in the area.
- **Set in order** — a place for everything; everything in its place.
- **Shine** — clean as you go; leave each area better than you found it.
- **Standardise** — same layout, same labels, every shift.
- **Sustain** — make it a daily habit, checked by the leads.

## 4. Daily tidying
- Return every tool to its labelled home after use; nothing left on benches.
- Break down and remove packaging; put consumables back in their store.
- Keep aisles, walkways and **all exits clear** at all times.
- Coil hoses/cords safely; no trip hazards or trailing leads.
- Empty and re-line waste bins; nothing overflowing.
- Clear plant debris off floors and benches to bins (not left to attract pests).
- Wipe down and clear work surfaces at the end of each task.
- Tidy PPE stations; restock gloves/paper; hang garments properly.

## 5. Weekly tidying
- Reset each tool board/station to its standard layout; return strays.
- Organise storage: rotate stock (first-in-first-out), face labels out.
- Break down cartons; take out recycling and general rubbish.
- Tidy and wipe office, break and locker areas.
- Check nothing is being stored on the floor that shouldn't be (pest/flood risk).
- Ensure cleaning equipment (mops, brushes) is stored clean, dry and off-floor.

## 6. Monthly tidying
- De-clutter: remove obsolete, broken or unused items from every area.
- Review storage layout & labelling; relabel anything faded/unclear.
- Inspect for and clear pest harbourage (clutter, debris, cardboard nests).
- Tidy exterior around entries/waste store; keep vegetation/rubbish clear of
  walls and screens.
- Confirm signage (room IDs, hazard, hygiene, directional flow) present & legible.

## 7. Standards & expectations
- **Clean-as-you-go** is the default working style, not an end-of-day task.
- Storage areas are labelled and assigned; no "temporary" piles.
- Documents/records filed in their place, not left loose in production areas.
- Chemicals and consumables stored per label, segregated, off the floor,
  away from food/drink and out of grow rooms unless in active use.
- Report (don't ignore) anything broken, leaking or out of place → maintenance
  log.

## 8. Verification
Leads do a quick walk-through each shift against this SOP; GM spot-checks weekly.
A tidy facility is part of audit readiness — it should always be
"inspection-ready."
