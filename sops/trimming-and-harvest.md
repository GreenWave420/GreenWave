# SOP: Trimming & Harvest Handling

**Doc ID:** SOP-TRM-001 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Version:** 1.0 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Owner:** GM / QA
**Effective date:** ____________ &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Review due:** annually

---

## 1. Purpose
To harvest, trim, dry and cure flower in a hygienic, consistent, fully-tracked
way that protects quality and cannabinoid/terpene content and keeps every batch
segregated and accounted for.

## 2. Scope
From harvest cut through trimming, drying, curing and transfer to storage.
Covers wet-trim and dry-trim workflows.

## 3. Batch control & traceability
- Every harvest = a **batch** with a unique ID. Never co-mingle batches on a
  table, rack or bin.
- Record wet weight at harvest, weights at each stage, trim/waste weights, and
  final dry weight. Reconcile against the tracking system.
- Label everything: genetics, batch ID, harvest date, stage.

## 4. Hygiene & setup (before every session)
- Trim room cleaned & sanitised; surfaces, tables and floor.
- Operators gowned: clean scrubs, hair covered, **gloves**, no jewellery/nail
  polish, no fragrances.
- Tools (scissors, blades, trays, bins, scales) sanitised and laid out.
- Scales calibrated/checked. Labels and batch record ready.
- Separate, labelled bins staged for: **trim (keep)**, **stem/waste**, **larf**.

## 5. Harvest
1. Confirm plants are at target maturity (trichome check: mostly cloudy, some
   amber — to your product spec).
2. Optional pre-harvest flush per feed plan.
3. Cut plants/branches; move to trim area without dropping material on the floor.
4. Weigh and record **wet weight** against the batch record.
5. Keep the batch physically segregated at all times.

## 6. Trimming
**Wet trim** (trim then dry) or **dry trim** (dry then trim) — follow the method
chosen for the batch; don't mix within a batch.

1. Remove large fan leaves, then sugar leaves, to product spec.
2. Handle buds gently — minimise trichome loss; don't crush or over-handle.
3. Keep trim (sugar leaf) for extraction separate from stem/waste.
4. **Clean resin build-up off scissors regularly** and swap to sanitised tools
   as they gum up.
5. Place trimmed flower onto labelled drying racks / into bins — do not overfill;
   keep airflow.
6. Reconcile trim + waste + product weights before leaving the batch.

## 7. Drying
| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Temp | 16–20 °C |
| RH | 55–62 % |
| Airflow | Gentle, indirect — never blowing directly on flower |
| Light | Dark |
| Duration | ~7–14 days (to ~10–12% moisture / stems snap) |

- Inspect **daily for mould/botrytis**; remove affected material immediately and
  record.
- Don't rush drying with heat — it degrades terpenes and quality.

## 8. Curing
1. Once dried to target, place flower in clean, sanitised airtight containers,
   filled ~⅔.
2. **Burp** containers daily for the first 1–2 weeks (open to exchange air /
   release moisture), then taper.
3. Monitor internal RH (target ~58–62%); pull and re-dry if too wet (mould risk).
4. Cure typically 2–4+ weeks to product spec.
5. Keep batch labelling intact throughout.

## 9. Waste & reconciliation
- Weigh and record all stem/leaf/waste; render regulated waste unusable and
  destroy per licence method, **witnessed and documented**.
- Final reconciliation: wet weight → dry weight → trimmed product + trim + waste.
  Investigate unexplained variance.

## 10. Transfer to storage
- Package/label finished product with batch ID and record the movement.
- Store per approved conditions (cool, dark, controlled RH, secure).
- Sample for required lab testing (potency, micro, contaminants) per plan.

## 11. Post-session
- Segregate and store all product; nothing left out.
- Clean and sanitise all tools, tables, floors and bins.
- Complete and sign the batch record.

## 12. Records
Batch record (all stage weights, dates, operators), waste destruction log,
test results, storage/movement log.
